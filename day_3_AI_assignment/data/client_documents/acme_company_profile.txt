ACME CORP – COMPANY PROFILE

Acme Corp is a mid-sized logistics company headquartered in Bangalore.

Acme has been a client for three years and currently uses our analytics platform.

Their main business areas are logistics, supply chain management, and fleet operations.

The primary client contact is Sarah Johnson, VP of Operations.

Acme is currently evaluating an expansion of the analytics platform to additional regions.

Their major concerns are pricing, implementation time, and reporting capabilities.

Acme currently has approximately 500 employees across its operations.

The company wants better visibility into fleet performance, delivery efficiency, and operational costs.

The client is interested in advanced analytics and dashboard capabilities.
